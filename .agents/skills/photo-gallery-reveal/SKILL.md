# Photo Gallery Reveal

This skill guides the creation of a dynamic photo gallery tailored for a romance website.

## Steps

1. Organize photos in a masonry or alternating grid layout.
2. Use GSAP ScrollTrigger or Intersection Observer to trigger animations.
3. Animate the photos by scaling them from `0.9` to `1` and changing opacity from `0` to `1`.
4. Apply a soft box-shadow and a subtle sepia or warm filter on hover to enhance the romantic feel.
